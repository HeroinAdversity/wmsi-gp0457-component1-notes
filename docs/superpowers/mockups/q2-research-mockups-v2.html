Source 3 · A student's projectWMSI MIRROR · Q2-M-0612-A

For my business studies course, I wanted to find out how much food restaurants waste. I emailed a small café near my school and arranged to interview the owner's nephew, Daniel, who was helping out during the school holidays.

I told Daniel the café would not be named, and he agreed that I could take notes. He had worked there for two weeks. We talked at the counter at lunchtime, so he often stopped to serve customers. Daniel said they threw away "about three bags" of food every night, but he did not know what was in them because the cook emptied the bins. From what he told me, I decided that restaurants across the country waste far more food than they used to.

2(b) "Young people waste more food than older people." Explain how this claim could be tested.

Answer scheme · 2(a) indicative content unlocked after your attempt
Strengths
  • Clear aim (amount of food wasted) keeps the research focused.
  • Primary, first-hand source from inside a food business.
  • Anonymity offered, so answers may be more honest (ethical).
  • Notes taken, so there is some record of what was said.
Weaknesses
  • One café: sample too small and not representative of "restaurants across the country".
  • Interviewee is a temporary helper of two weeks with limited knowledge; the cook handles the bins.
  • "About three bags" is an estimate, not a measurement: inaccurate.
  • Constant interruptions, so answers may be incomplete.
  • No data over time, yet it concludes "more than they used to".
  • One method only, so there is no triangulation.
