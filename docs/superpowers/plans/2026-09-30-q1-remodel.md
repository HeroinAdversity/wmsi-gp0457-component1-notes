- Build a **Q1 bank** like the Q2 one. It has 11 reworded past papers (one per session) and 22 mirror papers (two per session). Each item holds Source 1, Source 2 and every tag the games and worksheets need.
- The Q1 games:
  - 1(a) **Pinpoint**: pick the exact answer from distractors.
  - 1(b) **Name it**: name the statement type. **Signal word**: tap the words that give the type away.
  - 1(c) **Which level?**: global, national, local or personal. **Element sort**: tag a phrase as an issue, value, cause, consequence or action.
  - 1(d) **Which test?**: crowd, hurt, fair, domino or stuck. **Level up**: pick the edit that lifts a Level 2 judgement.
  - A **Daily mixed round** across all of them.
